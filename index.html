Why Gramo

They count what you log.
We count what you eat.

Gramo is a calorie-tracking utensil that automatically weighs every bite — no manual logging, no guesswork, nothing to remember.

⚖️

Weighs Every Bite

A high-precision load cell built into the spoon measures each spoonful as you eat — mass and calories, per bite, automatically.

📸

Two-Stage AI Recognition

Snap a photo of your plate. A two-stage recognition process identifies the dish and maps it to calorie data — accuracy that improves with every meal.

📈

Trend Insights

The app turns raw bites into trends — intake over days and weeks — so balanced eating becomes visible, not vague.

Companion App

Your meals,
decoded.

Gramo syncs with the app over Bluetooth and the cloud. Photo recognition, calorie logging, and intake trends — free tier included, with a Premium AI nutrition agent for deeper guidance.

  • Automatic sync via Bluetooth — zero extra steps
  • Photo-based dish recognition and calorie logging
  • Intake trends and eating insights over time
  • Runs in the browser — no installation required

Precision Hardware

Engineered down
to the gram.

Gramo's name comes from "gram" — the unit it was built to master. Inside the handle: custom firmware and chipsets, a load cell, and low-power Bluetooth, working as one sensing platform.

  • High-precision load cell, dual-sourced for supply resilience
  • Custom firmware on a Nordic BLE chipset platform
  • Real-time bite data streamed to phone or browser
  • End-to-end chain: spoon → app → cloud → insight
