Smart Spoon Project

Food recognition + intake tracking, in one lightweight eating experience.

Gramo is a concept smart spoon that combines food recognition, weight sensing, and meal summaries to make diet logging easier.

View demo How it works

1. Recognize food

Upload or take a meal photo. The app returns candidate dish names, confidence, and calories per 100g.

2. Measure intake

The spoon records weight changes and motion events while the user eats.

3. Generate summary

The app combines confirmed food data with intake weight to estimate meal calories.

Prototype chain

Phone sees, spoon measures.

Camera recognition estimates what the food is. Hardware sensing estimates how much was actually eaten. The result is a more complete meal record.

Photo/API → Dish + kcal/100g
Load cell/IMU → Intake events
App → Confirmed meal report
