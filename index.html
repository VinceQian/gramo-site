Our Core Features

Why Choose DiveGuard?

Combining cutting-edge technology with practical design, DiveGuard brings professional-grade underwater safety to everyone.

Underwater Acoustic Network

Real-time data sharing between divers and surface stations using advanced acoustic transmission technology. Stay connected even in zero visibility conditions.

Vital Monitoring

Continuous tracking of heart rate, oxygen saturation, water depth, and temperature. Get instant alerts when vitals exceed safe thresholds.
