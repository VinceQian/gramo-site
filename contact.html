Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

When will DiveGuard be available for purchase?

We're currently in the pre-production phase and planning for market launch in Q2 2025. Sign up on our mailing list to be notified when pre-orders open.

What will be the price range?

Our goal is to offer DiveGuard at 60-70% less than existing professional systems. Final pricing will be announced closer to launch, but we're targeting accessibility for recreational divers.

Can I become a beta tester?

Yes! We're recruiting experienced divers for our beta testing program. Contact us with your diving credentials and we'll get back to you about opportunities.

Do you offer bulk discounts for dive centers?

Absolutely! We have special partnership programs for dive centers, training facilities, and tour operators. Contact us to discuss volume pricing and integration support.
