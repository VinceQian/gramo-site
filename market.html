Who We Serve

Two segments, one need:
effortless accuracy.

🏃

Weight-Management Adults

People actively trying to lose weight and stay healthy. They already track — or tried to — and need convenient, accurate recording that doesn't demand daily discipline.

🌿

Nutrition-Focused Older Adults

Middle-aged and older adults seeking balanced nutrition and longer, healthier lives. They care about meal management but won't wrestle with complicated apps.

Early adopters: where we start.

📱

Calorie-App Veterans

People who have used apps like MyFitnessPal — 30M+ active users who already understand tracking, and already know its pain. Gramo removes the manual labor they hate.

🩺

Dietitians & Clinics

Weight-management clinics need verified intake data. There are 115,000 registered dietitian nutritionists in the U.S. (CDR, 2025) — we estimate ~1,000 reachable via dietetic communities and campus programs within 6 months.

Business Model

Hardware once.
Value every meal.

🥄

Gramo Device — $49

Bill of materials $28, a 43% gross margin. The spoon is the entry point: affordable enough for anyone, precise enough to be trusted. The load cell — our most critical component — is dual-sourced from two suppliers to hedge supply-chain risk.

✨

Freemium App — $4.99/mo Premium

The free tier covers food recognition and calorie logging. Premium unlocks the AI nutrition agent. Passive measurement drives retention — the core assumption that makes recurring revenue viable.
