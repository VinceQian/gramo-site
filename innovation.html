Core Features

What Makes DiveGuard Special

Five revolutionary features that transform underwater diving into a safer, smarter experience.

Acoustic Communication

Sends short data messages through sound waves, even in low visibility. Our proprietary acoustic modulation ensures reliable transmission up to 500 meters in open water.

  • Works in zero visibility conditions
  • Up to 500m range in open water
  • Low-frequency for better penetration

Vital Monitoring

Tracks heart rate, oxygen saturation, water temperature, and depth in real time. Instant alerts when vitals exceed safe thresholds.

  • Heart rate monitoring
  • Oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Depth & temperature sensors

Smart Distance Detection

Measures the range between team members at the press of a button. Keep track of your dive buddy and maintain safe distances during group dives.

  • Real-time distance tracking
  • Proximity alerts
  • Multi-diver tracking

Modular System

Wrist-worn unit pairs seamlessly with surface buoys and underwater relay nodes. Scale from solo dives to complex multi-team operations.

  • Wrist module + surface buoy
  • Expandable relay network
  • Scalable architecture
