Innovation

Everyone tracks what you log.
Gramo tracks what you eat.

The breakthrough isn't a better app — it's moving the measurement into the utensil itself, so tracking happens with zero extra steps.

🥄

Per-Spoonful Measurement

Gramo is the only tool that measures mass and calories per spoonful, as you eat. Kitchen scales stop at the plate; apps stop at estimates. Gramo measures the bite itself.

🤖

Two-Stage AI Recognition

A two-stage recognition process identifies dishes from a photo and maps them to calorie data. The database starts narrow and accurate — then grows smarter with every meal logged.

⚡

Zero Extra Steps

No manual entry, no pre-meal weighing ritual, no discipline required. You eat; Gramo logs. Effortless end-to-end tracking is the entire design goal.

🔗

Hardware–Software–Data Chain

Custom firmware and chipsets in the spoon, Bluetooth sync to the app, cloud services behind it — one seamless pipeline from sensor to insight.

🧠

A Moat That Compounds

The more meals you eat with Gramo, the more accurate detection becomes — and the higher the switching cost. Usage itself deepens the advantage.

📈

Retention by Design

Nutrition apps lose 86% of users. Our target: under 50% abandonment in the first 100 days — because passive measurement removes the reason people quit.
