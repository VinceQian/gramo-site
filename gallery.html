From Concept to Prototype

Model Gallery

Our journey began with a simple question: Can we make underwater safety affordable for everyone?
From sketches to working prototypes, each stage brought us closer to realizing that vision.

Every Prototype Dives Deeper into Possibility

Each iteration represents countless hours of design, engineering, and testing. See how DiveGuard evolved from initial concepts to a fully functional underwater communication and safety system.

Concept
✏️

Initial Sketches

Early conceptual drawings exploring wrist-worn form factors and surface buoy relay systems. These sketches laid the foundation for our modular approach.

CAD Design
📐

3D CAD Models

Detailed mechanical designs showing internal component layout, sensor integration, and waterproof sealing mechanisms. Precision engineering for harsh environments.

Prototype v1
🔧

First Prototype

Initial working prototype with basic acoustic transmission and sensor integration. Validated core concept and identified key areas for improvement.

Testing
🏊

Pool Testing

Controlled environment testing to validate waterproofing, communication range, and sensor accuracy. Multiple iterations refined the design.

Prototype v2
⌚

Refined Wrist Module

Enhanced wearable design with improved ergonomics, longer battery life, and optimized sensor placement. IP68 waterproof rating verified.

Field Test
🌊

Ocean Trials

Real-world testing in coastal waters. Validated performance in varying conditions including waves, currents, and marine interference.

Component
📡

Surface Buoy

Floating relay station with GPS tracking and cellular connectivity. Bridges underwater acoustic signals to surface monitoring systems.

Integration
🔌

System Assembly

Complete system integration showing wrist module, buoy, and base station working together. Full network mesh topology demonstrated.

Current
✨

Pre-Production Model

Latest iteration ready for manufacturing. Incorporates all learnings from previous prototypes with polished industrial design and optimized performance.

Development Journey

Evolution Timeline

Prototype 1 → Prototype 2 → Pre-Production Model

2023 Q4

Concept Phase

Initial research and conceptual designs. Team formation and problem definition. Feasibility studies for acoustic communication underwater.

2024 Q1

Prototype 1

First functional prototype completed. Basic acoustic transmission working. Initial sensor integration. Proof of concept validated.

2024 Q2

Testing & Iteration

Extensive pool testing. Identified waterproofing challenges. Refined sensor accuracy. Improved battery efficiency by 40%.

2024 Q3

Prototype 2

Enhanced design with improved ergonomics. IP68 waterproof rating achieved. Successful field tests in ocean conditions. Multi-device networking functional.

2024 Q4

Pre-Production

Final design refinements. Manufacturing partnerships established. Regulatory compliance testing. Product certification in progress.

2025

Market Launch

Production ramp-up. Initial market release. Customer feedback integration. Global distribution planning.
