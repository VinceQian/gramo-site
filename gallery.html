Design Philosophy

Technology that belongs
on the table.

Every curve of Gramo is shaped by one rule: it must feel like tableware first and technology second. Seamless surfaces, balanced weight, food-safe materials — the electronics disappear into the experience.

🥄

Familiar Form

Gramo's silhouette follows classic flatware proportions, so using it requires zero learning curve.

🧼

Made for Daily Meals

The enclosure is being designed around food-safe materials and a sealed, easy-to-clean handle — engineered from confirmed internal component dimensions.

🤏

Balanced by Design

Electronics and battery are laid out to keep the spoon's balance where your fingers expect it — technology that feels like tableware.
